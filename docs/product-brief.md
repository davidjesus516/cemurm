# CEMURM — Product Brief for Beta Users

## What is CEMURM?

CEMURM is a web app that helps musicians organize their songs and setlists. Think of it as a digital music binder that lives on your phone, tablet, or laptop — and works even without internet.

Whether you gig solo or play in a band, CEMURM keeps your repertoire in one place. You can create songs, build setlists for shows, transpose keys on the fly, and share everything with your bandmates.

## Who is it for?

- **Solo musicians** who need a reliable setlist tool for gigs
- **Bands** who want to collaborate on repertoire and share arrangements
- **Music teachers** who organize songs by difficulty or theme
- **Worship leaders** who manage rotating song libraries
- **Jam session regulars** who want quick access to chord charts

## Key Features

### Song Management
- Create songs in ChordPro format (text-based chords + lyrics)
- Organize by artist, genre, or custom tags
- Search your entire library instantly

### Setlists
- Build setlists for gigs, rehearsals, or practice
- Drag to reorder songs
- Add notes per song (e.g., "intro in Bb", "extend solo")

### Stage Mode
- Fullscreen, high-contrast display for live performance
- Swipe or use keyboard/pedal to navigate between songs
- Transpose any song to any key in real time
- Works offline — no Wi-Fi needed at the venue

### Collaboration (Coming in Hito 3)
- Share setlists with bandmates
- Real-time updates when someone edits
- Leave comments and annotations on songs

### Public Library (Coming in Hito 4)
- Browse community-contributed songs
- Discover new arrangements and chord charts
- Contribute your own arrangements

## What to Expect from Beta

CEMURM is in **active development**. During the beta period:

- **Core features work**: You can create songs, build setlists, and use Stage Mode
- **Some features are missing**: Collaboration, public library, and integrations are coming later
- **You may encounter bugs**: That's expected! Please report everything you find
- **Your feedback shapes the product**: We prioritize features based on beta user input

### Current Beta Scope (Hito 1–2)
- ✅ Sign up / sign in (email, Google, GitHub)
- ✅ Create and edit songs (ChordPro format)
- ✅ Build and manage setlists
- ✅ Stage Mode with transposition
- ✅ Offline support
- Following hitos: Collaboration (Hito 3) and Public library (Hito 4) — not in this beta scope, coming later

## Our Promise

We're building CEMURM because we're musicians too. We know what it's like to fumble with paper setlists in the dark, lose arrangements in email threads, and deal with apps that don't understand how musicians actually work.

CEMURM will always be:
- **Free for core features** — song management and setlists
- **Offline-first** — it works without internet, because venues don't have Wi-Fi
- **Community-driven** — your feedback decides what we build next
- **Respectful of your content** — you own your songs, not us

Thank you for being part of the beta. Your feedback makes CEMURM better for every musician.
